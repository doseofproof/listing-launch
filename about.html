I’m Maddie Brass, a home and lifestyle creator in Fort Smith. Brand work — product demos, cozy routines, the posts that do not look like ads — lives at ugcbymaddie.com and on @maddie.brass.

Listing Launch is the real estate version of that work. Everyday UGC is problem-then-solution: the thing that removes a small daily annoyance. A house can be cut the same way. A hidden pantry is not a wide shot of cabinets. It is the counter that stays clear. A mudroom is not a hallway. It is where the morning stops falling apart.

I am not a licensed agent, I do not own a brokerage, and I do not own Moved by Maddie. If you need representation, that is a different hire. If you need the listing to move on a phone screen, that is this one.

Shoots are based in Fort Smith and booked through the River Valley and Northwest Arkansas — Bentonville, Rogers, Fayetteville, Springdale, and the towns between.
