On set in about 15–20 minutes of talking

You will not have to memorize a script.

The thing that keeps agents off video is the camera, not the house. The shoot is built so you only ever say two sentences at a time.

01

Pick the hook

Curiosity, a price-to-value claim, or a call-out. We choose before anyone is in the driveway.

02

Micro-chunks

I feed each line. You deliver it. Dead air gets cut later, not suffered through on set.

03

Move

Walk toward the phone or through a doorway. Stiff shoulders read as an ad. Motion reads as a person.

04

Captions on top

Most people watch muted. Bold top-frame text carries the line if the sound is off.

Hook menu

The first three seconds are the product.

Curiosity

Open a loop.

“You will not believe what this Greenwood house is hiding in the back.” “Three things the MLS photos missed.”

Price to value

Say the number.

“Stop scrolling — this is what $425,000 gets you in Fort Smith right now.” Useful when the house punches above the price.

Call-out

Name the buyer.

“If you want Bentonville without an HOA, stay.” “Moving to Rogers? This is the Tuesday version.”

Timeline

From hold to post.

  1. You send the address, price, and the one thing a buyer should feel.
  2. I send two hook options and a loose shot list the day before.
  3. On site: stills if it’s a hybrid, then vertical, then your lines.
  4. Raw clips land the same day for Stories.
  5. Edited reels, captions, and audio links follow on the agreed turnaround — standard is next day, rush if the open house cannot wait.
