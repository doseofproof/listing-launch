01 — The shift

MLS photos wait. The feed does not.

Horizontal HDR stills still belong on the listing. They do not start conversations. Short-form video puts the house in front of people who were not searching Zillow that hour.

Legacy delivery

A folder of stills.

  • Wide rooms, empty of the agent.
  • Horizontal files for the portal.
  • No hook, no audio, no caption.
  • The buyer has to already be looking.

Listing Launch

A distribution kit.

  • Vertical reels with a first-three-seconds hook.
  • You on camera, coached, not abandoned.
  • Captions, trending-audio links, raw B-roll.
  • Something to show in a listing presentation.

02 — What ships

Three jobs. Three cuts.

Every Hybrid Launch is built as a set, not one long tour chopped into pieces.

Discovery · 15–30s

The hook tour

Opens on the feature or the price-to-value claim. Made to be found by people nearby who were not hunting listings.

Authority · 20–45s

The agent walkthrough

You in the doorway, two lines at a time, a clear ask. This is the clip that makes the listing feel like yours.

Share · 10–15s

The lifestyle cut

Fast detail, beat-matched. The pantry, the light on the stone, the mudroom that actually solves the morning.
